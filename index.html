WHY THIS EXISTS

Good ideas should not get stuck at the technical part.

Most people can describe what they want to build. Fewer enjoy figuring out hosting, DNS, email records or how to make a site look credible. This offer exists to carry that part for you.

You bring the idea, the words and any images you already have. I help turn them into a clean website and guide the launch. If you already have an HTML file from another tool, I can host that too.
